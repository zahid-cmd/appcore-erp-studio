Job Card Rules
The charge of a service estimate : a customized service (Service Setup > Customized Services) with its rate and its income ledger. It is collected on the estimate when the customer does not take the service; when the estimate becomes a job card, a collected charge comes off the service invoice. An estimate is printed only when the charge was collected or a job card was made from it.
About the Workshop Setup
  • A bay is a work place of a service center; a job card can name the bay where the bike is worked on.
  • A technician is an employee of a department under the Service Center wing (Human Resource Setup > Department > Wing). One technician can work on more than one job card; every start and stop is time stamped.
  • The checklist items are copied to every new job card, in their order.
  • An observation is a problem of a bike (seen, or told by the customer); a diagnosis is what was found. Both are picked on a service estimate and go to the job card as its complaints. Users with the page action Add Observation (Service Estimates) can add new ones from the estimate.
  • More rules (estimate required, parts issue without requisition) come with their pages.
