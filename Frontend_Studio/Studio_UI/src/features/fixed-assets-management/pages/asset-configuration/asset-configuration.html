How the module works
  • Saving an asset group makes its own two ledgers : the asset ledger and the accumulated depreciation ledger. Nothing is picked.
  • The depreciation of an asset group is declared on Depreciation of Fixed Assets, before assets are bought into it.
  • Depreciation is charged for the full month an asset is put to use, and not in the month it is disposed.
  • Every posting is a Journal Voucher.
  • Money is paid or received on Payment / Receipt Vouchers against the payable / receivable ledger.
  • Opening assets post nothing, because they are already in the ledgers.
