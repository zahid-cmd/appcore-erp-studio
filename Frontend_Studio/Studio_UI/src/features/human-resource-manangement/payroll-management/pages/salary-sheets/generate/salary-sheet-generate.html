How the salary is worked out
  • Every employee of the branch with a salary structure gets a line (set the salary on Employee Profiles).
  • Unpaid days are absent days, unpaid leave, days not marked, half of each half day and the days before joining. Their share of the salary is cut.
  • Late : with 3 here, every 3 late days cut one day of Basic. 0 means late days are not cut.
  • Overtime is not part of the salary sheet : it is worked out with it and shown on the Overtime Sheet of the month once the sheet is posted.
  • The structure's deductions (provident fund, tax ...) and the salary advance installments are taken off : only advances paid out on Payroll Payments by the month end are recovered.
  • On the Draft sheet you can add another earning or deduction for anyone, then approve and post it.

Mark the attendance of the whole month first : a day that is not marked is not paid.
