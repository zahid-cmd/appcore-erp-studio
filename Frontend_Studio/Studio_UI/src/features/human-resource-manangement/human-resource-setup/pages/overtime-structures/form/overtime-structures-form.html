  • Overtime is paid on two bases : extra hours (typed on Daily Attendance) and extra days (days worked on a weekly holiday or a public holiday).
  • Example of extra days : a month of 30 days with 4 weekly holidays and 2 public holidays has 24 working days. Attending 28 days is 4 extra days.
  • The hours a day are not typed : they are the working hours of the employee's shift (shift hours less the break).
  • The structure is picked on the Employee Profile when Overtime allowed is on. The salary sheet works the overtime out from it.
