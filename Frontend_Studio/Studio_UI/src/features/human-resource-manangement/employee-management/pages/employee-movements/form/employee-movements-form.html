How it is used
  • Saving a movement changes the employee record at once and keeps what was before.
  • An increment is a new gross salary, split by the employee's salary structure (or another one picked here). A salary sheet made afterwards uses the new salary for its whole month.
  • Separation makes the employee Inactive. The days after the last working day are not paid. The final settlement here is a note; pay it with a Payment Voucher.
  • A movement is not edited. Delete the employee's latest movement to take it back.
