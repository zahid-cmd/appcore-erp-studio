How it works
  • A member can borrow up to {{ number(policy.LoanMaxPercent) }} % of the own part, less what is still on loan, in no more than {{ policy.LoanMaxInstallments }} instalments (Provident Fund Policy).
  • Interest on a new loan : {{ policyInterestText }} (Provident Fund Policy). It is income of the fund; a loan keeps the terms it was given with.
  • An instalment is taken from the salary sheet of every month from the start month, until the loan is back. A loan settled early pays the interest of the months used only.
  • A loan does not lower the fund balance : what is still out is taken off the payment when the member is settled.
  • A loan is not edited. One nothing was recovered of can be deleted.
