How it works
  • The member always gets the own part. The company part is decided by the policy from the reason and the years : nothing is typed.
  • Pick "Membership Cancelled" when the employee stays in the company but leaves the fund.
  • Saving makes the member Settled : no more fund deduction is taken from the salary. Pay the net amount with a Payment Voucher.
  • A settlement is not edited. Deleting it on the list makes the member Active again and takes the payment and the forfeiture back.
